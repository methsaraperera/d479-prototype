Practical Information

Getting Around

  • Public Buses: 5am-11pm daily ($5)
  • Taxi: Available in Taniti City
  • Car Rental: Near airport with local agency
  • Bikes: Available for rent (helmets required by law)

Practical Information

  • Currency: USD (also accepts EUR, JPY)
  • Power Outlets: 120V (US standard)
  • Hospital: One main hospital + several clinics
  • Language: English spoken in cities and tourist areas

Important Notes

  • Alcohol: Not served/sold midnight-9am
  • Drinking Age: 18 years old
  • Crime: Very rare; watch for petty theft
  • Holidays: Many attractions close on national holidays
